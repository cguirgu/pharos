# Coptic Orthodoxy — Content Foundation (research notes)

## The Agpeya — Book of Hours (7 canonical hours)
"Seven times a day I praise You" (Ps 119:164). Each hour ties to an event in Christ's life.
1. **Prime / Morning (6am)** — the Resurrection; the true Light rising
2. **Terce / Third Hour (9am)** — descent of the Holy Spirit; Christ's trial
3. **Sext / Sixth Hour (noon)** — the Crucifixion
4. **None / Ninth Hour (3pm)** — Christ's death in the flesh; the repentant thief
5. **Vespers / Eleventh Hour (sunset)** — evening raising of incense
6. **Compline / Twelfth Hour (bedtime)**
7. **Midnight (three watches)** — the Second Coming (Gethsemane)
+ Prayer of the Veil (clergy/monks).
Structure of each hour: Lord's Prayer → Prayer of Thanksgiving → Psalm 50 → selected Psalms → Gospel → Litanies → "Lord Have Mercy" x41 (39 lashes + crown of thorns + spear) → conclusion. Prostrations (metanoias) counted on a prayer rope (mequteria). Pray facing East.

## Fasts (Copts fast ~210–240 days/yr; vegan; abstain food+water midnight→sunset, then one vegan meal)
- **Great Lent** (~55 days, before Pascha/Resurrection) — strictest, holiest
- **Nativity / Advent Fast** — Nov 25 → Jan 6 (43 days; seafood allowed exc. Wed/Fri)
- **Apostles' Fast** — variable, after Pentecost → July 12 (feast of Sts Peter & Paul)
- **St. Mary's / Dormition Fast** — Aug 7 → 21/22 (15 days)
- **Fast of Nineveh (Jonah)** — 3 days, ~2 weeks before Lent
- **Paramon fasts** — day(s) before Nativity & Theophany
- **Every Wednesday** (betrayal of Judas) **& Friday** (crucifixion) — except the 50 joyful days after Resurrection and when a major feast falls
- Fasting framed as joy/privilege preparing for the Feast, not hardship.

## Feasts
- **7 Major Feasts of the Lord**: Annunciation, Nativity (Jan 7), Theophany/Baptism, Palm Sunday, Resurrection (Pascha), Ascension, Pentecost
- **7 Minor Feasts of the Lord**: Circumcision, Entry into the Temple, Flight into Egypt, etc.
- **Nayrouz** — Coptic New Year (~Sept 11); celebrates the martyrs
- **2 Feasts of the Cross** (Tout 17 / ~Sep 27; Baramhat 10 / ~Mar 19)
- **St. Mary feasts**, **Feast of the Apostles**, Passover of Jonah
- **Monthly commemorations**: 12th — Archangel Michael; 21st — St. Mary the Theotokos; 29th — Annunciation/Nativity/Resurrection

## Synaxarium
Daily lives of saints & martyrs — on average ~3 martyrs commemorated each day. "Saint of the day" content.

## Identity & motifs
- **Church of the Martyrs** — Coptic calendar (Anno Martyrum) begins AD 284 (Diocletian). 13 months (12×30 + 5/6 days).
- Coptic cross, the Pharos (lighthouse) of Alexandria, lampstand, desert monasticism (St. Anthony, St. Paul the hermit), Pope Shenouda III, St. Athanasius, St. Mark (founder, See of Alexandria).
- Tasbeha (midnight praises), liturgical hymns/tunes.
