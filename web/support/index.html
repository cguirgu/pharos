Support

Questions, problems, or feedback about Coptic Daily Companion — we're happy to help.

Contact us

Email us at support@cgsoftwarestudio.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 1–2 business days. Include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem — it helps us reproduce it quickly.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. You can use Coptic Daily Companion without one — choose “Continue without an account” on the welcome screen. Your rule, journal, and progress stay on your device. An account is only needed to sync across devices.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app: You → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all synced data.
  • How do I export my data? In the app: You → Export my data.
  • How do I manage reminders? In the app: You → Reminders, or your device's notification settings.
