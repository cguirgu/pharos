Dear Fr. Samuel,

My name is Christina Guirguis. I build a free iPhone app called **Coptic Daily Companion**: a
daily companion for Coptic Orthodox practice — the fasts and feasts of the Coptic calendar, the
Agpeya, the daily readings, and a personal rule of life. It is open source, and the code is at
github.com/cguirgu/pharos.

The most common request I get from users is for the saint of the day.

I am writing because your parish's English translation of the Synaxarium is, as best I can
determine, the text I would be showing them. It has been digitised and passed around online —
there is a public dataset on Codeberg (`randogoth/coptic-synaxarium`) covering all 366 days that
credits St. George Coptic Orthodox Church, Chicago as the source of the English. That dataset
carries only a note saying "free to use," which is the author's word and not the Church's. So I
would rather ask you than assume.

**What I am asking permission for:** to display your English text of the day's Synaxarium reading
inside the app, one day at a time, to whoever opens it that morning.

**What I am committing to, whatever you decide:**

- The app is free. There is no subscription, no advertising, and the Synaxarium would never sit
  behind a payment of any kind.
- The text would be shown one day at a time, inside the app only. No bulk export, no separate
  republication, no resale.
- Attribution in whatever words you prefer — on the screen where the reading appears, not buried
  in a settings page. If you would like it to read differently from "English translation:
  St. George Coptic Orthodox Church, Chicago," just tell me the wording.
- If you ask me to remove it, at any point and for any reason, I will, in the next release. You
  would not need to explain.
- If you or anyone at the parish spots an error in how the text is rendered, I will fix it.

**What the app does today, while I wait to hear from you:** it shows only *which* saints and
feasts fall on each Coptic day — the commemorations themselves, which are a matter of the
Church's calendar. The written lives are switched off in the code and do not reach the screen.
They stay off unless you say yes. If you would rather I not show even the commemoration titles as
they are worded in your translation, tell me and I will take those out too.

I know this is not the most pressing thing in your inbox, and I am not asking for a quick answer.
A simple yes or no is all I need, and no is a perfectly good answer — I would just point people
to your published volumes instead.

Thank you for the work of the translation itself. It is a real gift to the English-speaking
Church, and it is the reason this is possible at all.

Asking your prayers and your blessing,

Christina Guirguis
