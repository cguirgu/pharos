**Subject:** Permission to use the copticchurch.net Synaxarium in a free Coptic Orthodox app

---

Dear friends at CopticChurch.net,

My name is Christina Guirguis. I build a free, open-source iPhone app called **Coptic Daily
Companion** — the Coptic calendar's fasts and feasts, the Agpeya, the daily readings, and a
personal rule of life. The code is public at github.com/cguirgu/pharos.

The feature my users ask for most often is the saint of the day, and your per-day English
Synaxarium at copticchurch.net/synaxarium is the cleanest and most usable version of that text
on the web. I would like your permission to use it.

I want to be straightforward about why I am writing rather than simply using it. Your site does
not carry a copyright notice or terms of use. I am aware that several open-source projects have
taken that silence as permission and scraped the text. I do not think silence works that way —
copyright is automatic and needs no notice — so I would rather have a real answer from you than
a convenient assumption.

**What I am asking for:** permission to display your English Synaxarium inside the app, one day
at a time.

**What I commit to, either way:**

- The app is free — no subscription, no advertising, and the Synaxarium would never be paywalled.
- One day at a time, in-app only. No bulk export, no separate republication, no resale.
- Attribution in whatever words you specify, on the screen where the reading appears — with a
  link back to copticchurch.net if you would like one.
- Removal on request, in the next release, no explanation needed.
- Any error you spot, I fix.

If the text is not yours to license — if you are yourselves publishing it by arrangement with a
translator or another parish — I would be grateful if you could point me to whoever does hold it.
That would be just as useful to me as a yes.

Thank you for keeping this text freely available online for as long as you have. It has been a
resource for a great many people.

In Christ,

Christina Guirguis
